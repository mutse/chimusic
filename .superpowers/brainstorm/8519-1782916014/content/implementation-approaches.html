Implementation Approach

We are building option A from the previous screen: a local-first NetEase Cloud Music-style player, across mobile and desktop, with no account, login, cloud sync, membership, comments, follows, or social feed.

A

Recommended: Product Cleanup + NetEase-Style Local Discovery

Keep the current Flutter architecture and playback/storage foundation. Remove visible account, cloud, Pro, and AI surfaces. Retitle tabs and cards around NetEase-like local experiences: Discover, Search, Library, Now Playing, Daily Mix, Recently Played, Albums, Artists, Favorites, and Play History.

B

Visual Rewrite First

Rebuild the major screens to look closer to NetEase Cloud Music before cleaning internals. This gives a dramatic UI change quickly, but leaves account/cloud/AI concepts tangled in state and tests for longer.

C

Deep Domain Refactor First

Remove user/cloud/AI models and repository fields first, then rebuild UI. This is architecturally tidy, but the app may stay visually unchanged for a while and the blast radius is larger.

Why A

  • Matches the existing controller, repository, and tests.
  • Delivers visible product progress quickly.
  • Keeps local playback history as a first-class feature.
  • Lets deeper model cleanup happen safely after behavior is covered.

Tradeoff

  • Some unused service files may remain briefly until follow-up cleanup tasks remove them.
  • The first pass prioritizes user-facing correctness over perfect internal minimalism.
