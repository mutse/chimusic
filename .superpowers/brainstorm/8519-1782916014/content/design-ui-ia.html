Discover

NetEase-like home surface driven by local files: daily local mix, continue listening, recently imported, most played, favorites, albums, artists, and folder playlists.

Search

On-device search only. Results group tracks, albums, artists, folders, and saved playlists. Recent searches stay local. AI labels, quotas, and Pro prompts are removed.

Library

Local collection manager: tracks, albums, artists, folders, favorites, saved collections, unavailable files, and import actions. Sort and filter stay lightweight.

Player

Mini player and full player keep album art, queue, shuffle/repeat, waveform/progress, lyrics state when available, like/save actions, and local history updates.

Visual direction

Use NetEase-inspired red accents, dense music surfaces, strong album-art hierarchy, and rounded playback controls, while keeping the current ChiMusic glass system only where it helps clarity. Remove profile/cloud/AI decorations that compete with playback.
